How this is modelled
  • Traffic. Each driver follows the improved IDM with the constant-acceleration heuristic, acts at their own reaction points (0.3 to 1.0 s) and is occasionally distracted. There are no lanes: riders and drivers move sideways into whichever gap moves fastest, as on Hosur Road. The mix is 56% two-wheelers, 24% cars, 10% autos, 6% BMTC buses and 4% trucks. Buses stop at the kerb for 15 to 40 s, some autos stop for passengers, people cross on foot, and a few riders jump the red. The signal shows 40 s green, 3 s amber, 77 s red.
  • Who sees what. Without the app, a driver notices a hazard 28 to 55 m ahead, less when a car or bus blocks the view. With the app, a warning can arrive hundreds of metres ahead.
  • Radio. Each Bluetooth advertising event reaches a phone with a chance that falls to 50% at the radio reach. A bus or truck in the path cuts that by 30%, and a busy channel cuts it further. Origins send 10 events and relays send 4, 100 ms apart, as the app does.
  • Mesh rules are ported from the RoadMesh app, core/mesh/MeshEngine.kt (road profile). A warning can be relayed 4 times and stays fresh for 5 s. A relay waits up to 200 ms (less for distant receivers) plus 0 to 20 ms of jitter, and is dropped after the phone overhears 2 copies further along. Each phone relays at most 3 frames and originates at most 2 per second. A warning is spoken within 400 m when its origin is ahead within 30°. Long-lived reports are re-sent every 20 s within 250 m. Road queries follow RoadQueries.kt and the advice follows RoadPicture.forecast.
  • What warned drivers do. They move out of a reported pothole or crash up to 200 to 250 m early, slow to 20 km/h for a pothole they cannot avoid, and slow to 25 km/h near a crash. For a slowdown they ease off once to the spoken speed, v × D / (D + 90 m), never below 15 km/h, and hold it for 30 s. These are assumptions about people, not measurements.
  • The comparison is fair. Applying the mesh forks the road into two copies with the same vehicles, arrivals and driver randomness, and incidents land in both. The copies then differ only because of the mesh. With every driver response switched off they stay identical, which check.mjs verifies.
  • Not calibrated against field data. Use it to compare designs under identical conditions, not to forecast real numbers.
